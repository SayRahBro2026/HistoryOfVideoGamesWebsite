GTA6


Grand Theft Auto VI is an open-world, action-adventure game developed and made by Rockstar Games, released on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.

The Grand Theft Auto (GTA) series began in 1997 as a top-down 2D project originally called Race 'N' Chase, developed by Scottish studio DMA Design (now Rockstar North).

Grand Theft Auto (GTA) is popular because it combines unexpected open-world freedom, ironic storytelling, living city design, and long-term multiplayer support that turn every release into a massive cultural event

GTA 5 was made in 2013, which means it took them about 13 years to make GTA6!
