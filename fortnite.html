Fortnite


Fortnite is a large multiplayer FPS. It was made by Epic Games and has gained millions of players. It even holds the Guinness World Record for the most players at one single time, with 14 million people

Fortnite was first released in 2017 with only one game mode: Battle Royale, and it quickly became one of the most popular types of game modes ever. As time went on, it released more game modes like Reload, Save The World, and even custom-made maps that players have made.

Fortnite's popularity is mostly because of the fact that is was first of its time. Its unique style of multiplayer FPS made it unique from other games of its time.
