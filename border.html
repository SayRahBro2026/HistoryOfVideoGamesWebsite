Borderlands

Borderlands is a franchise about a planet with a hidden vault no one has found, but everyone wants to find themselves.

Borderlands 1

In Borderlands 1, you play as one of 4 vault hunters who are on a planet called Pandora to uncover a box vault. This random lady called the Guardian Angel guides you along the way of trying to find the vault.
Along the way, you meet a wide range of people you can kill. Bandits, Corpos, and an army who works for those corpos.
Will they piece together a key to open a vault?
Will they even reach the vault?
Of course they do. That's why there's 7 more games.
They enter the Vault Of The Destroyer. And surprise surprise, there's an ancient alien monster.
After the vault hunters kill that alien, they release toxic waste into the enviroment, called Eridium, which erids the ecosystem of its original form.

Borderlands 2

After 5 years, new vault hunters are trying to hunt vaults. But, they are ambushed by Handsome Jack who believes his ambush killed all the hunters.
Then, this annoying robot called CL4P-TP, or Claptrap, helps the hunters survive. Then, the Guardian Angel comes back.
The Hunters join a resistance group against Handsome Jack and his company that rules over Pandora called the Crimson Raiders.
Remember the Guardian Angel? She's Handsome Jack's daughter. And to make things worse, she's a siren. The second worst thing is the fact Handsome Jack uses her to charge the Vault Key, but who cares. The hunters sure do!
They kill the guardian angel so she isn't tortured anymore. Handsome Jack uses his handsomeness to kill one of the leaders of the Crimson Raiders, and keeps the other one to charge the key.
They rescue the surviving Crimson Raiders leader, and kill Handsome Jack. But guess what? He unleashes yet another ancient monster.
Also, there's more vaults across the galaxy. Why are there so many vaults? No one knows!

Borderlands 3

Several years after Handsome Jack was murdered, there is a violent cult led by twins who want to take the power from the vaults.
Lilith, the Crimson Raider leader who survived, formed a new group of 4 vault hunters. This time, they are aboard a spacecraft to venture through planets for vaults.
They go through 3 planets to get pieces of a key for one planet, fighting corperate people and the Violent Cultists.
After getting the keys, the Leader of the Violent Cultists kidnap a scientist, who has the Guardian Angel's powers. They rescue her by going back to Pandora.
Now here's the plot twist that no one EVER would've guessed except the people who say "I've been saying that this entire time!"
Pandora...
IS THE GREAT VAULT ITSELF!!!!
One of the leaders of the cult dies while opening Pandora, and the other absorbs his energy and forms with the Destroyer. Who is the same alien from Borderlands 1, except in that game it wasn't the real deal.
Then, somehow, the Vault Hunters kill the last cult leader. Remember Lilith, the Crimson Raider leader? She sacrifices herself to stop the moon-key thing that opens Pandora from crashing into Pandora. Now, the galaxy is changed.

Borderlands 4

After six years from Borderlands 3, we shift focus to Kairos, a hidden planet. Not so hidden anymore, cause when Lilith stopped the moon, it revealed Kairos.
Guess what? The vault hunters still join the Crimson Raiders. This time, it's to stop this guy named the Timekeeper and his synthetic army.
The Crimson Raiders enlist 3 factions to join them to stop the Timekeeper. There's also a vault here too. But to get to it, they have to kill the Timekeeper's Lieutenants.
One of them is experimenting on Siren's to get Lilith, who isn't actually dead, but trapped in another dimension. They also meet a Siren, who isn't a real siren, but is an artificial siren, who wants to get revenge on Lilith.
After killing the lieutenants and saving Lilith, they kill the Timekeeper, who is a Vault Guardian. Inside the vault, instead of immeasurable amount of wealth, it's Lilith, from the future, who gives the hunters a cube and then disappears.

Theres 4 spinoff games, and Borderlands: The pre-sequel, But I don't have time to cover any of those.
