Pokemon Go


Pokemon Go is an augmented reality game, mixing real-life locations with the virtual Pokemon world. Catch Pokémon by clicking on them and swirling your Poké Balls to curve them and catch!

Pokemon Go was made on April 1, 2014, and was a partnership of Niantic and Game Freak. It was widely popular during the COVID pandemic, and caused people to start going outside and walking.

Sadly, because of the crowding and traffic, that Pokemon Go ended up getting banned in many areas.
