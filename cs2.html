Counter-Strike

Counter-Strike is a more than 27 year old First-Person Shooter where Counter-Terrorists have to stop terrorists from planting a bomb.

GoldSrc

Counter-Strike 1.6 is one of the oldest versions available to purchase on steam. It's assets are very... Old, to say the least. Despite it's oldness, it is still a solid game.
Counter-Strike: Condition Zero is another one of the oldest CS games. Following 3 developer changes for a reason beyond me, the game is fundamentally the same as CS-1.6, with the addition of a solo campaign. Now, this campaign might seem interesting, but trust me, its pretty much not. Its just doing tasks while killing enemy bots with friendly bots doing so little it might be an easter egg that there is a 70% chance they are spies for the enemy. (Can you tell I'm not a big fan of the campaign?)

Source

Counter-Strike Source is the first of two games on the source engine. The game is no different than CS:CZ, but that may be because I've play Condition Zero on PC and I play CS-Source on my phone. Not much to talk about.
My favorite Counter-Strike game to play in chinese, its Counter-Strike: Global Offensive! Contrary to the name, this game was not globally offensive. This game was the main CS game for about 11 years. An addition of 5 weapons, my favorites being the MP5-SD (300 kills on my stat-trak Kitbash) and the R8 Revolver,gambling with in-game crates for a chance to hit it big and win (for example) a Karambit Case-Hardened pattern 387 which is probably worth hundreds of thousands, and most importantly, cheats. Cheats has been a thing for a long, long time in Counter-Strike, with the need for Valve Anti Cheat way back in 2002. Can't forget about the game, though. The graphics look gritty and realistic, but overshadowed by your nerves, and your teammates yelling at you during a 1v4.

Source 2

The hip, new, and super duper cool source edition brought Counter-Strike 2! Which, instead of looking hyper realistic and super gritty, its semi cartooney and more colorful. The fire looks like real fire, the smoke looks like real smoke. For old PC's it's like "Oh there's a guy dead ahead of you? Oh I'm sorry I can't handle this I have to take a break."(I've died to my pc stuttering more times than you'd pray would happen to your worst enemy.) The cheats are still here, however. Thank god Valve's Anti Cheat appropriately called VAC is here to help! Right? Right? RIGHT???? During the early stage of CS2 being a thing, cheats were rampant. Remember how I mentioned the R8 Revolver was my favorite gun in CSGO? Well, in the early stages of this Counter-Strike game, they buffed the gun to make you to jump around while spinning with perfect accuracy. Doesn't seem to work with me, though. Now, on to premier. Premier is a gamemode locked behind 15 dollars, and about 100 hours of play time. Unlike the rank system of other games, in CS2, premier rating is based off of wins. For me, I've played about 30 premier matches, and I only have 7 wins. That is about it.
